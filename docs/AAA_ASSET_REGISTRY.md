## 2. Licensing and Compliance Standards

- **Original Creation:** All 3D meshes, procedural shaders, and textures are original implementations tailored to the Wayfarer game architecture.
- **Zero Proprietary GTA Assets:** No proprietary assets from Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive are used. All models are original fictional vehicles, buildings, and characters inspired by California and Pacific Northwest architecture.
- **Unreal Engine 5 Forward Compatibility:** All vehicle attachment transforms, character bone hierarchies, and PBR textures conform to Unreal Engine 5 asset conventions for future engine bridges.
